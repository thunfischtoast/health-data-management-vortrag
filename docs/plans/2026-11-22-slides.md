## Constraints
- Opens by double click on `index.html` (file://), no internet, no build step, no module scripts, no `fetch`. Classic scripts only. Vendored reveal.js 6.0.2 in `vendor/reveal/` (`reset.css`, `reveal.css`, `reveal.js`, `plugin/notes.js`). Never edit vendor files. No further dependency.
- Slides in English; invented clinical documents, PDF crops and the demo in German, with English callouts. Plain English, no em dashes, no "not X but Y", no triads for effect. Sentence-case headings. Few words on the slide, the rest in speaker notes.
- UKM design: 1920x1080 (template is 960x540 pt, multiply by 2). Arial. Colours `#003866` dark blue, `#006DB0` UKM blue, `#7DB6D7` light blue, `#80A1C8`, `#00A0F0`, text `#2C3F48`, white. One signal colour: highlighter yellow `#FFE571`, used only for "this is the source". Conflict/limit: `#C31F38`, sparse. Chart series: `#006DB0` and `#7DB6D7` with direct value labels.
- Geometry from the template (pt): content slide: logo 30,20 61x40 (`assets/ukm/logo_blue.svg`); header line right-aligned 525,30 (talk short title) and 525,49 (name and date), 12 pt; title 69,106 width 822; body 69,199 822x269; slide number 891,501, 16 pt; small arc bottom right 604,358 356x182 (`arc_small_blue.svg`, check its viewBox and crop). Check fill and stroke rendering, the SVGs contain their own colours.
- Minimum text size on slides 24 px at 1920 (source lines 20 px). Body 34 px or larger where possible.
- Animation: reveal fragments only advance steps. One function in `js/scenes.js` computes the number of visible fragments and sets `data-step` on the current section (events ready, slidechanged, fragmentshown, fragmenthidden). CSS derives everything from `data-step`, so stepping back and jumping in (`#/5/0/2`, `fragmentInURL: true`) give the right state. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Duration 300 to 600 ms.
- Every evidence figure has a short source line on the slide (author, venue, year). Full citation with URL, design, n and caveat goes into the notes, taken from the JSON (`claim`, `detail`, `fix` notes below). Invented material carries a small label "invented example".
- Chapter indicator: slim bar at the bottom with four labels (Status quo, New methods, RACOON-RAVEN, Outlook), current one highlighted, hidden on title, divider and closing slides.
